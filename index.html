🪪 Chinese Name Registration Guidelines
Already have a Chinese name?
If you already have an established/official Chinese name from Level 1 or Level 2, you may skip this step and continue using your existing Chinese name!

For students WITHOUT a Chinese name:

Please provide Remi老师 with your preferred short name based on the following rules:

  • Must be easily recognizable from your official Full Name (as on your Matric Card).
  • Ideally 2 to 3 syllables long (e.g., David ➔ 大卫 Dàwéi; Anna ➔ 安娜 Ānnà; Siti ➔ 茜蒂 Xīdì).
  • Remi老师 will phonetically transcribe and assign standard Chinese characters for you!
