💡 Cultural Note: Surname Comes FIRST in Chinese Names!

Unlike Western/English names (First name + Last name), Chinese names always place the Family Name (Surname) FIRST, followed by the Given Name.
E.g., In my name Yáng Zǐxián: Yáng is the family surname, and Zǐxián is the given name!

7. Getting Your Custom Chinese Name

Already have an official Chinese name?

If you already have an established/official Chinese name from Level 1 or Level 2, you may skip this step and continue using your existing Chinese name!

🏷️ For students WITHOUT a Chinese name:

Please provide Remi lǎoshī with your preferred short name based on the following rules:

  • Must be easily recognizable from your official Full Name (as on your Matric Card).
  • Ideally 2 to 3 syllables long.
  • Example: If your full name is Ahmad Daniel bin Mohd Azlan, your short name can be Daniel.

✨ Remi lǎoshī will then phonetically match and select meaningful Chinese characters to craft your custom Chinese name!
